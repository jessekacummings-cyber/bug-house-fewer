Let’s explore fewer!

  1. Choose 5–10 bugs and 1 fewer or 2 fewer.
  2. Count the bugs in the house together. Turn on Build from empty to move the starting bugs into the ten-frame one by one.
  3. Tap or drag bugs out into the garden. Move one for 1 fewer or two for 2 fewer.
  4. Count the bugs left in the house. Write the number on the board or type it below.
  5. Choose Show how many are left to reveal and check the count.

Tap a garden bug to bring it back. The pen, eraser, undo, and clear buttons work on the writing board. New number starts another round. Reset round starts the current round over.
